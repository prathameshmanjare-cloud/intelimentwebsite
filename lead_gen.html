Capture — get the email
High-priority forms and overlays
Must-have
Inline content upgrade
An opt-in box embedded mid-article, offering a bonus resource tied to that exact post.
"Get the free checklist for this guide → [Email] [Download]"
Must-have
Sticky footer bar
A slim bar pinned to the bottom of the viewport with a one-line value prop and email field.
"Join 12,000 readers. Weekly insights, no spam. [Subscribe]"
Must-have
Exit-intent popup
Triggers when the cursor moves toward the browser bar — last chance to capture before they leave.
"Wait! Get our free ebook before you go → [Yes, send it]"
High impact
Content gate
Show 60% of a long article, blur the rest. Subscribe to unlock the full piece.
"This article is free for subscribers → [Continue reading]"
Smart CTAs — context-aware prompts
Triggered by scroll depth, topic, or behavior
Contextual
End-of-post CTA
After the article ends, readers are primed — place a topic-matched subscribe prompt here.
"Get our weekly SEO playbook → [Subscribe free]"
Contextual
Lead magnet card
A visually distinct card mid-page or sidebar promoting a free resource (PDF, template, tool).
"Free: 50 email subject line templates → [Get the PDF]"
Smart
Scroll-triggered slide-in
Appears from bottom-right after user scrolls 60–70% down, signaling genuine interest.
"Enjoying this? Get more like it → [Yes please]"
Smart
Quiz or assessment
Multi-step interactive quiz — results gated behind email. High engagement, high conversion.
"What's your marketing maturity score? → [Get results]"
Trust — overcome hesitation
Social proof and transparency signals
Trust signal
Social proof counter
Show subscriber count or logos of well-known brands/companies in your audience.
"Trusted by 24,000 marketers at Google, HubSpot, Shopify"
Trust signal
Testimonial snippet
A 1–2 sentence quote from a real subscriber near the opt-in form to reduce signup friction.
"This newsletter changed how I run my agency — [Name, Founder]"
Transparency
Sample issue preview
Link to a past newsletter so users know exactly what they're subscribing to before deciding.
"Here's a recent issue → [Preview] before you decide"
Urgency
Scarcity block
Time-limited offer or limited-seat cohort to push wavering visitors to act now.
"Doors close Friday. 47 spots left in the free cohort → [Join now]"
Engagement — keep them coming back
Retention, community, and referral loops
Retention
Related posts widget
3 curated article cards at the end of every post, matched by topic to increase pages-per-session.
"You might also like → [card] [card] [card]"
Community
Comment / reaction bar
Quick emoji reactions or a short comment prompt requiring account signup to participate.
"Was this useful? Leave a comment (free account)"
Interactive
Poll / vote widget
Embed a one-question poll mid-post. Results viewable after voting — keeps users engaged.
"Which tool do you use most? [Option A] [Option B] [Vote]"
Referral
Share + refer block
Post-subscribe page with a referral link — unlock bonus content by referring friends.
"Refer 3 friends → unlock the full swipe file [Copy link]"
Convert — move leads down the funnel
High-intent actions and personalization
High intent
Book a call CTA
For service businesses — embed a Calendly widget or button after high-intent content.
"Want us to do this for you? → [Book a free 20-min call]"
Personalized
Preference selector
Let subscribers pick their topics at signup — increases relevance and open rates later.
"What are you most interested in? [SEO] [Ads] [Content]"
Nurture
Free tool / calculator
Embed a useful calculator or generator — results delivered to email, capturing the lead.
"ROI calculator → [Run the numbers] → email me my results"
Social
Tweetable insight block
Pull quote styled as a shareable card with a one-click tweet button to drive organic traffic back.
"80% of leads go cold after 5 days → [Tweet this]"
Pro tip
Don't stack more than 2–3 of these on a single page. The highest-converting combo for blogs is: inline content upgrade (mid-post) + end-of-post CTA + scroll-triggered slide-in. For newsletters, add a sample issue preview and a subscriber count near every opt-in form.
